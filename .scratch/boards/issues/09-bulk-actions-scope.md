## Question

Should v1 support bulk actions on a board — multi-select add from search results, or moving/copying an item from one board to another in one step?

## Answer

No — ruled out of scope for this spec. Adding/removing items stays strictly one-at-a-time (quick-add icon per row, search-and-link one result at a time in the Boards view), consistent with every other add/remove interaction already decided (quick-add icon, search bar). Revisit only as a future effort if the single-item flow proves too slow in practice.
